FAQs

How does Arabela rental work?

∨

Arabela Gown Rental is a web-based reservation and inventory management system designed to make renting a gown simple and hassle-free. Here is how the process works step by step:

1. Create an account. Register on the website to get started. Your account keeps track of all your reservations and rental history.

2. Browse the gown collection. Explore the full catalog of gowns online. Each gown shows its real-time availability status — Available, Reserved, or On Rent — so you instantly know if a gown is free without having to message or call the shop.

3. Use AI Recommends. Not sure what to pick? Use the AI Recommends feature — just tell it your event type and preferences, and it will suggest the most suitable gowns available in the collection for you.

4. Submit your reservation. Once you have chosen a gown, submit your reservation directly through the platform. No walk-in or Facebook message required.

5. Pay the security deposit. After submitting, you will be directed to the payment page to pay the Php 2,000.00 security deposit via GCash or cash. This confirms your booking.

6. Pick up your gown. You may pick up the gown at the shop two days before your event during business hours (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM).

7. Return the gown. Return it to the shop within two days after your event. Once the gown is returned on time and in good condition, your security deposit will be refunded to you.

Once your payment is verified, open the reservation from your Reservations page — the Order Summary & Receipt panel there shows the gown, rental dates, amount breakdown, and the payment proof you uploaded, all tied to your reference number. That is your official record of the transaction.

How do I make a reservation?

∨

Log in to your account, browse the gown collections, and click on the gown you want. You can filter by event type (Wedding, Debut, Graduation, etc.) to narrow down your options. Once you select a gown, submit your reservation through the platform. You will then be directed to the payment page to pay the security deposit.

What is the AI Recommends feature?

∨

The system has an AI Recommends feature that helps you find the right gown. It will ask you about your event type (wedding, debut, graduation, or other formal occasion) and your preferences. Based on your answers, it will suggest the most suitable gowns available in the inventory — so you don't have to scroll through everything manually.

How about the security deposit?

∨

A security deposit of Php 2,000.00 is required for every reservation. This is separate from the actual rental fee of the gown. You will get the full deposit back when you return the gown on time and in good condition. If there are late return penalties, the amount will be deducted from your deposit.

What are the payment options?

∨

The system accepts GCash and cash payments only. For GCash, send the payment to Arabela Gown Rental's registered GCash number and upload a screenshot as proof of payment on the platform. For cash, you may pay in person at the shop. The admin will review and verify your payment before the reservation is confirmed.

When can I pick up and return the gown?

∨

You may pick up the gown two (2) days before your event date, and you must return it two (2) days after the event. This is the standard pickup and return window. All pickups and returns must be done in person at the shop during business hours (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM). The system does not offer delivery or courier service.

What if I return the gown late?

∨

If you return the gown outside the allowed two-days-after window, a penalty of Php 200.00 per day will be charged. This penalty will be deducted from your security deposit. The system automatically flags reservations as Overdue once the return deadline has passed.

Will I receive a receipt or rental agreement?

∨

Yes. Once the admin verifies your payment, open your booking from Reservations — the Order Summary & Receipt panel is your receipt: itemized gown, rental dates, amount breakdown, and the payment proof you uploaded, tied to your reference number. The rental terms themselves are covered in our Terms & Conditions, which apply to every booking.

What happens if I cancel reservations often?

∨

Cancellation attempts are recorded on your account — both cancelling a reservation you already submitted, and holding a selection then leaving without submitting it (cancelling the countdown or letting it run out). Once you reach fifteen (15), your account is automatically flagged for review by our staff. A flagged account can still browse and reserve, but staff will review it before confirming further bookings. An admin can lift the flag.

Can I reserve multiple gowns?

∨

Yes, you can make as many reservations as you wish. Just keep in mind that cancellation attempts are recorded — including selections you hold and abandon — and fifteen (15) or more will automatically flag your account for staff review. Completing the deposit payment for each reservation keeps your account in good standing.
