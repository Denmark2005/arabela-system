ARABELA GOWN RENTAL

How it works

STEP ONE

FIND YOUR PIECE

Browse our full collection of gowns and suits online. Use the AI Recommends feature to find the perfect outfit for your event — just tell it your occasion and preferences and it will suggest the best options for you.

image
image
STEP TWO

RESERVE IT

Once you found your piece, submit your reservation directly on the website. You can select your event date during reservation. For size, you may choose your size if you already know it — but if you are unsure, you can leave it as TBD (To Be Determined) and inform the staff at the shop when you pick up. Your reservation will be on Pending status until you complete the security deposit payment.

STEP THREE

PAY THE SECURITY DEPOSIT

Pay the Php 2,000.00 security deposit through the website via GCash only. Upload your proof of payment so the admin can see and verify it. This is required — the admin cannot process and confirm your reservation without it.

image
task_alt

Admin Verification

STEP FOUR

WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION

Once the admin verifies your GCash payment, your reservation status will be updated and the gown will be officially Reserved under your account. Your Order Summary & Receipt — reachable from Reservations — becomes your official record, showing the amount breakdown and the payment proof you uploaded.

STEP FIVE

PAY THE FULL RENTAL AMOUNT

The remaining full rental amount is paid separately when you visit the shop or via GCash on the day of pickup.

payments

Final Payment Process

STEP SIX

PICK IT UP

Visit the shop two (2) days before your event date to pick up your gown. Business hours are 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM. If your size was set to TBD, let the staff know your size upon arrival so they can assist you with the fitting.

STEP SEVEN

WEAR IT.

Look and feel your best on your special day — whether it's a wedding, debut, graduation, or any formal occasion.

image
STEP EIGHT

RETURN

Return the gown to the shop within two (2) days after your event. Make sure it is in good condition to get your full security deposit back. Late returns: Php 200.00 / day
