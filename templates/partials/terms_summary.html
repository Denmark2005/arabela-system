The 7 things that matter most

    Security deposit. {{ site_deposit_php_short }} for every gown. It comes back to you after the return, less any fees and damage.

    Cancelling a confirmed booking. Once a booking is confirmed, its security deposit is not refunded. Anything you paid over the deposit is returned.
