You are responsible for the gown while it is in your possession. Take good care of it as if it were your own. Do not attempt to wash, iron, or alter the gown in any way.

Arabela Gown Rental shall not be liable for failure to perform any of its obligations due to reasons beyond its reasonable control, including fire, earthquake, natural disasters, and other force majeure events.

03Extra Days and Late Returns

Extra days

Need the gown longer? You may choose an earlier pick-up or a later return. Each booked day outside the free days costs {{ site_late_fee_php }} per gown.

  • The fee is taken from your security deposit when the gown is returned. Nothing extra is paid when you book.
  • It is charged for the dates on your booking, even if you pick up later or return earlier, because the gown is kept for you on those days.
  • The order summary, your booking page and your receipts show the amount before and after you book.

Late return

Each day the gown comes back after your booked return date costs {{ site_late_fee_php }} per gown, on top of any extra days fee, and is taken from your security deposit. Returning within two (2) days after your event is never late.

Fees above the deposit

If the fees and deductions are more than your security deposit, you pay the difference at the shop.

Booked dates cannot be changed on the website. To change your pick-up, return or event date, call Arabela{% if site_phone %} at {{ site_phone }}{% endif %} with the booking number on your receipt.

  • Our staff can change your dates only if the gown is free on the new days, including its five (5) cleaning days after the return.

05Security Deposit

A security deposit of {{ site_deposit_php }} per gown is required for every reservation. This must be paid through the website via GCash only. You must upload your proof of payment on the platform for the admin to verify. The admin cannot confirm your reservation without a verified deposit payment.

After the gown is returned and checked, your security deposit is given back to you less any extra days fee, late return fee, and damage or loss charges. When every fee and charge is zero, the full deposit is refunded. Your Deposit Return receipt lists every deduction.

Privacy: the picture of your GCash receipt is read automatically by Google's Gemini service to check the payment before the shop verifies it.

06Damage, Loss and Laundry

All gowns are professionally cleaned and pressed before every rental. Each gown is inspected and prepared in ready-to-wear condition upon pickup. You will receive the gown clean, pressed, and in good condition.

Upon return, do not wash, dry clean, or attempt to clean the gown yourself. Arabela Gown Rental handles all cleaning and laundering after every rental. Simply return the gown as is after your event and our team will take care of the rest.

  • If the gown is returned with stains, tears, or any form of damage beyond normal wear, our staff will check it and the cost of the repair will be deducted from your security deposit. If the repair costs more than your deposit, the balance will be discussed with you at the shop.
  • If the gown is lost, the amount to be deducted depends on the gown and will be agreed with you at the shop.
  • Always return the gown regardless of its condition so we can determine whether it can be repaired. You are liable for all repair and replacement costs.

07Rental Fee and Cancellation

Upon confirming a reservation, the full rental fee is to be paid separately on the day of pickup either at the shop in person or via GCash.

Cancellations

You may cancel your reservation through the website at any time before the admin confirms it, including a reservation that has already been confirmed and paid for, as long as it has not yet been picked up. Cancellation attempts are recorded on your account. Once a reservation is confirmed, its security deposit is not refunded if the reservation is cancelled, whether you cancel it or Arabela cancels it because the gown was not picked up. The extra days fee is not charged on top of it. The cancelled reservation stays on record at the shop, so please contact Arabela if you would like to book again.

Both of the following count toward that total, every time:

  • Cancelling a reservation you already submitted — at any stage, including one already confirmed and paid for and awaiting pick-up
  • Holding a selection and leaving without submitting it — whether you press Cancel on the countdown or simply let it run out

Repeated attempts lead to escalating, automatic consequences:

  • 5 attempts — your account is temporarily locked from starting a new reservation for 30 minutes
  • 10 attempts — a further temporary lockout of 2 hours
  • 15 attempts — your account is automatically flagged for review by our staff

A flagged account can still browse the collection and submit reservations, but our staff will review the account before confirming any further bookings. If you believe your account was flagged in error, please contact us and an administrator can lift the flag.

Reservation hold

When you proceed to the reservation page, your selection is held for twenty (20) minutes so that you can complete your details and pay the {{ site_deposit_php }} security deposit. A countdown is shown at the bottom of the screen while the hold is active. If the hold runs out or you cancel it before you submit, your selection is released and becomes available to other customers again — you are free to select your items and start a new reservation, subject to the cancellation limits above. If you choose a different item while a hold is already active, you will be asked to confirm: doing so releases your current hold (counted as a cancellation attempt, as above) and starts a fresh twenty-minute hold for the new selection.

Unpaid security deposits

Your reservation stays in Pending status until the {{ site_deposit_php }} security deposit is paid and verified by our staff. If your proof of payment is missing, you can upload it from your Reservations page while the reservation is still pending. Reservations left unpaid may be cancelled by our staff, and those cancellations count toward the fifteen (15) attempt review threshold described above.

08Size and Fitting

Please choose your size when you submit your reservation online. If you are not sure of your size, choose the size you think fits you best. You can then try the gown on at the shop, and our staff will assist you with the fitting.

Alterations: if the gown is too loose or too big for you, our staff can adjust it to fit your size.

Fitting and alterations can be done at the shop, within shop hours, up to one (1) week before your pickup date. Whether it can be done depends on whether the gown is available during that week, because it may still be with another customer, so please check with our staff first.

Arabela Gown Rental is not liable for size issues if the correct size was not communicated prior to or during pickup.

09Account, Status and Receipts

You are responsible for all activity under your account. Make sure your reservation details, event date, pick-up and return days, and contact information are accurate when submitting a reservation.

After submitting a reservation, it will remain in Pending status until you complete the GCash security deposit payment and the admin verifies it. Only after admin verification will your reservation be marked as confirmed and the gown officially reserved under your account.

Once your payment is verified, the Order Summary & Receipt panel on your reservation — reachable from Reservations — is your official proof of transaction: the gown, event date, pick-up and return days, amount breakdown, any amount to be taken from your deposit, and the payment proof you uploaded, tied to your reference number. Every payment and deposit return also gets a numbered receipt you can view and print.
