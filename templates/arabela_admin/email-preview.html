Setup help

Render's Free plan blocks the normal Gmail connection (SMTP), so the site needs one of these to send real email:

  1. Paid Render plan. Upgrade the service to any paid instance. Gmail then works as is, with EMAIL_HOST_USER (the shop's Gmail address) and EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD (a Gmail App Password) set on Render.
  2. Free: the Google relay. Open apps_script/mail_relay.gs in the project and follow the steps at the top of that file (about 5 minutes in your Google account). Then set on Render: EMAIL_BACKEND=accounts.email_backends.AppsScriptRelayBackend, EMAIL_RELAY_URL and EMAIL_RELAY_SECRET.

Then press Send test to me. When the test arrives in your inbox, set CUSTOMER_EMAILS_ENABLED=true on Render to start emailing customers. Google lets a normal Gmail account send about 100 emails a day through the relay (500 through SMTP), which is plenty for this shop.
