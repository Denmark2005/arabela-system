Included by all four templates that open a photo modal, so the behaviour can't drift between them. The problem this fixes: the viewer used to be a full-width box with a grey background, and the photo was centred inside it. A portrait gown photo (most of them) therefore sat in the middle with wide empty grey panels down both sides. The frame below is inline-block instead, so it shrinks to the exact size of whatever was uploaded -- the background is simply never visible beside the photo, at any aspect ratio. {% endcomment %}
