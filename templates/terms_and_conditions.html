Terms of service

Rental Agreement

The rental period begins when you pick up the gown from the shop two (2) days before your event date. The rental period ends when the gown has been returned to the shop within two (2) days after your event date and inspected by our staff to be in acceptable condition.

You are responsible for the gown while it is in your possession. You are expected to take good care of it as if it were your own. Do not attempt to wash, iron, or alter the gown in any way.

In the event that the gown is damaged, we will assess the item and inform you of the additional charges needed to repair it. Should the gown be lost or damaged beyond repair, you will be required to pay the full retail price of the item. Always return the gown regardless of its condition so we can determine whether it can be repaired at a lower cost versus the full retail price. You are liable for all repair and replacement costs and the charges will be deducted from your security deposit or billed to your account.

Arabela Gown Rental shall not be liable for failure to perform any of its obligations due to reasons beyond its reasonable control, including fire, earthquake, natural disasters, and other force majeure events.

Laundry and Gown Condition

All gowns are professionally cleaned and pressed before every rental. Each gown is inspected and prepared in ready-to-wear condition upon pickup. You will receive the gown clean, pressed, and in good condition.

Upon return, do not wash, dry clean, or attempt to clean the gown yourself. Arabela Gown Rental handles all cleaning and laundering after every rental. Simply return the gown as is after your event and our team will take care of the rest.

If the gown is returned with stains, tears, or any form of damage beyond normal wear, additional charges will apply and will be assessed by our staff upon inspection.

Security Deposit

A security deposit of Php 2,000.00 is required for every reservation. This must be paid through the website via GCash only. You must upload your proof of payment on the platform for the admin to verify. The admin cannot confirm your reservation without a verified deposit payment.

The full security deposit will be refunded to you upon return of the gown on time and in good condition. Any applicable penalties or charges will be deducted from your deposit before it is returned.

Rental Fee, Late Fee, and Cancellation

Upon confirming a reservation, the full rental fee is to be paid separately on the day of pickup either at the shop in person or via GCash.

If the gown is returned beyond the allowed two (2) days after your event date, a late return penalty of Php 200.00 per day will be charged. This will be deducted from your security deposit.

Cancellations

You may cancel your reservation through the website at any time before the admin confirms it. Cancellation attempts are recorded on your account, and once you reach fifteen (15) your account is automatically flagged for review by our staff.

Both of the following count toward that total:

  • Cancelling a reservation you already submitted
  • Holding a selection and leaving without submitting it — whether you press Cancel on the countdown or simply let it run out

A flagged account can still browse the collection and submit reservations, but our staff will review the account before confirming any further bookings. If you believe your account was flagged in error, please contact us and an administrator can lift the flag.

Reservation Hold
When you proceed to the reservation page, your selection is held for twenty (20) minutes so that you can complete your details and pay the Php 2,000.00 security deposit. A countdown is shown at the bottom of the screen while the hold is active. If the hold runs out or you cancel it before you submit, your selection is released and becomes available to other customers again — you are free to select your items and start a new reservation at any time.

Unpaid Security Deposits
Your reservation stays in Pending status until the Php 2,000.00 security deposit is paid and verified by our staff. If your proof of payment is missing, you can upload it from your Reservations page while the reservation is still pending. Reservations left unpaid may be cancelled by our staff, and those cancellations count toward the fifteen (15) attempt review threshold described above.

Pickup and Return Policy

All pickups and returns must be done in person at the Arabela Gown Rental shop only. Arabela Gown Rental does not offer delivery or courier services.

Shop Hours: 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Pickup: Two (2) days before your event date within shop hours.
Return: Two (2) days after your event date within shop hours.

Please make sure to arrive within shop hours. Pickups or returns outside of 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM will not be accommodated. Failure to return within the allowed period will result in a daily penalty of Php 200.00 deducted from your security deposit.

Size and Fitting

You may select your size when submitting your reservation online. If you are unsure of your size, you may leave it as TBD (To Be Determined). Upon pickup, inform our staff of your size and they will assist you with the fitting at the shop. Arabela Gown Rental is not liable for size issues if the correct size was not communicated prior to or during pickup.

Account Responsibility

You are responsible for all activity under your account. Make sure your reservation details, event date, and contact information are accurate when submitting a reservation.

If you accumulate fifteen (15) or more cancellation attempts — cancelled reservations or abandoned holds combined — your account is automatically flagged for staff review. An administrator can lift the flag once the matter is resolved.

Reservation Status

After submitting a reservation, it will remain in Pending status until you complete the GCash security deposit payment and the admin verifies it. Only after admin verification will your reservation be marked as confirmed and the gown officially reserved under your account.

Your Receipt and Rental Agreement

Once your payment is verified, the Order Summary & Receipt panel on your reservation — reachable from Reservations — is your official proof of transaction: the gown, rental dates, amount breakdown, and the payment proof you uploaded, tied to your reference number. This Terms & Conditions page is the rental agreement itself and applies to every booking.

Payment Methods

Security Deposit — GCash via website only.
Full Rental Fee — GCash or cash in person at the shop on pickup day.

No other payment methods are accepted.
